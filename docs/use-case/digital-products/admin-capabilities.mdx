Spree Commerce provides tools for managing digital products, offering flexibility and control over how files are configured, delivered, and accessed. Whether you're selling standalone digital goods or bundling them with physical items, Spree helps you streamline digital product operations while maintaining security and customer satisfaction.

Below is a breakdown of the key capabilities available to store administrators.

## Flexible Product Configuration

- Configure any product to be digital-only, physical, or a hybrid (e.g., physical textbook with downloadable companion PDF).
- Attach digital files (PDFs, ZIPs, etc.) to any product variant — including variants of physical products, so a manual or warranty document ships alongside the goods.
- Upload, replace and remove files from the **Digital files** card on the product page. Replacing a file updates it for everyone who already bought it; nobody loses access.

## Download Access Management

- Limit how long a download link stays valid and how many times it can be used, from **Settings → Store**.
- Override either limit on an individual file, so an evergreen download and a time-limited course can live in the same catalog. Leave a file's limits blank and the store settings apply.
- Restore a customer's access from the order page when they run out of attempts or a download failed.

## Delivery Step Bypass

- Digital products are delivered by the built-in Digital delivery method, which needs no shipping address.
- If a cart only contains digital items, the delivery step is skipped entirely for faster checkout.

## Automated Digital Delivery

- Digital items are fulfilled automatically when the order is placed — download links are ready immediately, with no manual step.
- Customers are emailed their download links as soon as the order is placed, and you can re-send that email from the order page at any time.
- Signed-in customers can also retrieve everything they have bought from your storefront or app, across all their orders.

## Download Visibility

- The order page shows each file a buyer received: how many downloads they have used, when access lapses, and whether it has run out.
- A `digital_link.downloaded` event fires on each successful download, so you can feed downloads into your own auditing, analytics, or fraud tooling via webhooks.

## Security and Permissions

- Files are stored privately and served through protected, time-limited download URLs.
- Each link is unique to a single purchased unit, and non-public file storage prevents direct linking or unauthorized downloads.

## Bundling & Promotions

- Bundle digital products with physical items or offer them as free gifts with purchase.
- Create promotions or discounts specific to digital goods.

## Custom Rules & Logic (Optional)

- Use Spree's flexible architecture to create custom workflows, such as:
  - Sending your own download emails by subscribing to order and download events
  - Generating personalized files (e.g., license keys or certificates)
  - Integrating with external file storage or DRM systems
