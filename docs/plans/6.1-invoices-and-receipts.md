# Invoices and Receipts

**Status:** In Review — the whole plan for 6.1 is built with `6.1-b2b-order-documents.md` in spree/spree#15080; the storefront part is spree/storefront#293, which waits for an `@spree/sdk` release with the new clients
**Target:** Spree 6.1
**Depends on:** `6.0-multi-vendor-marketplace.md` (seller orders, seller legal identity, payouts), `6.1-b2b-order-documents.md` (the order Documents card, private-storage files), `6.0-document-numbers.md` (why order numbers are not invoice numbers), `6.0-tax-provider.md` (`taxability_reason`, which Enterprise generation reads)
**Author:** Damian + Claude
**Last updated:** 2026-11-09
**Tracking:** Linear V-3773 · GitHub spree/spree#15064

## Summary

European merchants and marketplace operators need a legally valid
invoice or receipt for every sale, in the name of whoever sold the goods.
In a marketplace that is usually the seller, not the platform. Today
Spree has no place to keep one, no way to get it to the buyer, and no way
for a consumer to ask for one.

This plan adds the open-source half: one record, `Spree::Invoice`, for
every invoice, receipt and credit note on an order or a seller payout,
whoever made it. Staff, sellers and integrations upload them, by hand or
through the Admin and Seller APIs, from an ERP, an accounting tool, a
national e-invoicing platform or a seller's own software. Spree stores
them privately, shows them in one Documents card on the order, delivers
them to the buyer (storefront, Store API, email), records consumers'
invoice requests, and shows operators which paid orders still have no
invoice.

**Generating** invoices (numbering, the legal snapshot, PDF rendering,
automatic issuing, credit notes, commission invoices, structured
e-invoicing) is the Enterprise invoicing product, designed in
`ee/docs/plans/invoice-generation.md`. It creates the same
`Spree::Invoice` records, so everything in this plan works the same for
invoices Spree generated and invoices it received.

## Key Decisions (do not deviate without discussion)

1. **Open source holds and delivers invoices; Enterprise generates them**
   (2026-11-01). The record, uploads, storage, delivery to buyers,
   invoice requests and the missing-invoice view are open source.
   Numbering, the legal snapshot, PDF rendering, automatic issuing,
   credit notes, commission invoices, appearance settings and structured
   e-invoicing are Enterprise. The legal duty to issue an invoice is met
   by whichever system issues it, so the plumbing every merchant needs
   (somewhere to keep invoices, a way to reach the buyer, a way for the
   buyer to ask) is free, while Spree replacing a merchant's invoicing
   tool is a product. This is Saleor's split: a core invoice record with
   uploads, and generation as a separate app. It reverses an earlier
   version of this plan from the same day, which put generation in open
   source. **It supersedes the "no invoice vocabulary in OSS" part of the
   2026-08-30 decision** in `6.1-b2b-order-documents.md`. The other half
   of that decision, no PDF stack in core, still holds.
   - **Accepted trade-off:** Magento Open Source and Sylius generate
     invoices free, and Medusa's docs show how to build it. Open-source
     Spree answers the invoicing question with uploads and requests, and
     sells generation.

2. **One record for every invoice, however it was made.**
   `Spree::Invoice` has a `kind` (`invoice`, `receipt`, `credit_note`),
   an `issuer` (the store or a seller), a number, dates, totals and a
   private file. `source` says whether it was `uploaded` or `generated`.
   The open-source model has no snapshot and no number series: Enterprise
   keeps those in its own tables, linked to the invoices it generates, so
   no column here is ever empty because of where an invoice came from.
   One list, one CSV export, one storefront section for both.
   `Spree::OrderDocument` stays generic paperwork (the buyer's PO,
   specifications) and never holds an invoice.
   - **Rejected (2026-11-01):** uploaded invoices as `OrderDocument` rows
     with a `kind` column. With `Spree::Invoice` in open source, it would
     have split invoices across two models depending on who made them.

3. **The issuer is whoever sold the goods.** A seller order's invoices
   are issued by the seller. A first-party order's invoices are issued by
   the store. This follows the marketplace plan, where the seller is
   merchant of record for their seller order. When a seller is
   platform-remitted (`tax_remittance: platform`, the deemed-supplier
   case of Article 14a of the VAT Directive), the store issues instead. A
   commission invoice belongs to a seller payout and is issued by the
   store to the seller.

4. **Invoices are never edited; an upload can be deleted only until it is
   sent.** An invoice's number, totals and file never change. A mistaken
   upload can be deleted while `sent_at` is empty, meaning the buyer has
   not been emailed it. Once sent, a correction is a credit note.
   Generated invoices are never deleted.

5. **Invoice numbers are the issuer's, never Spree's order numbers.** An
   uploaded invoice carries the number its issuing system gave it, unique
   per issuer and kind. Order, payment and other document numbers are
   never invoice numbers (`6.0-document-numbers.md` says why). Legal
   numbering series are part of Enterprise generation.

6. **Consumers can request an invoice, whoever issues** (2026-11-01). A
   request is a `Spree::InvoiceRequest` on the order, holding the buyer's
   company name, VAT number and billing address. Spree publishes
   `order.invoice_requested`, shows the open request to staff or the
   seller on the order, and marks it fulfilled when an invoice of kind
   `invoice` is added to the order, uploaded or generated. Polish law
   entitles a consumer to an invoice on request within three months, so
   the request works for every seller. With Enterprise generation on,
   the invoice is issued at once.

7. **One Documents card on the order** (2026-11-01). The admin, the
   seller panel and the storefront show one card per order: Invoices
   (invoices, receipts and credit notes, with any open request) above
   Other documents (`OrderDocument`). Each section has its own upload
   action. `Spree::Invoice` and `Spree::OrderDocument` share their
   private-file handling (private storage, file type checks, expiring
   download links) through one concern, also used by tax exemption
   certificates and seller requirement submissions.

8. **Operators see which paid orders have no invoice** (2026-11-01). A
   calculated "Missing invoice" filter on orders (paid, and no invoice or
   receipt) and a per-seller count on the operator's seller page, with no
   status column. Amazon tracks invoices still to be uploaded per
   shipment, and VTEX will not mark an order invoiced without one; an
   operator of upload-only sellers needs the same view to chase them.

9. **Invoices are kept, even when a customer is anonymized.** VAT law
   requires keeping invoices for five to ten years, depending on the
   country. The GDPR right to erasure does not apply to data kept under a
   legal obligation (Article 17(3)(b)). `Customers::Anonymize` leaves
   invoices and their files untouched, as it already does for
   order-frozen tax identifiers.

10. **Built together with order documents** (2026-11-01). The first
    implementation also builds `Spree::OrderDocument` from
    `6.1-b2b-order-documents.md` (model, APIs, the Other documents section
    and the buyer's uploads), so the Documents card ships whole. The
    storefront part of both plans goes to spree/storefront as its own pull
    request, linked from the spree/spree one.

11. **Settings in open source are limited to delivery and requests**
    (2026-11-01). The store has `invoice_request_window_days` (default
    90) and `send_invoice_email` (default on). The store's legal identity,
    document appearance, series, issuing switches and the seller
    invoicing agreement are read only by generation, so they are
    Enterprise.

## Design Details

### Data model

```ruby
class Spree::Invoice < Spree.base_class
  has_prefix_id :invc                   # `inv` is taken by Spree::Invitation
  include Spree::SingleStoreResource
  has_private_document :file            # private storage, PDF only, size checked on disk
  publishes_lifecycle_events

  KINDS   = %w[invoice receipt credit_note].freeze
  SOURCES = %w[uploaded generated].freeze

  belongs_to :issuer, polymorphic: true                  # Spree::Store | Spree::Seller
  belongs_to :order, optional: true                      # exactly one of order / seller_payout
  belongs_to :seller_payout, optional: true              # commission invoices
  belongs_to :original_invoice, class_name: 'Spree::Invoice', optional: true  # credit notes, invoices replacing a receipt
  belongs_to :uploaded_by, polymorphic: true, optional: true

  # kind, source (set by Spree::Invoices::Create), number, issued_on, supplied_on
  # currency, net_total, tax_total, total    — decimal(19, 4), never floats
  # external_reference                        — the issuing system's id
  # sent_at
  # unique (issuer_type, issuer_id, kind, number)
end

class Spree::InvoiceRequest < Spree.base_class
  has_prefix_id :ireq
  belongs_to :order
  belongs_to :customer, class_name: Spree.user_class.to_s, optional: true
  belongs_to :billing_address, class_name: 'Spree::Address', optional: true
  belongs_to :invoice, optional: true          # the invoice that fulfilled it
  has_status :open, :fulfilled, default: :open
  # company_name, tax_id (normalized, at most 32 characters)
end
```

A credit note's `original_invoice` is optional, because an uploaded
credit note may correct an invoice issued before Spree held any. When set,
it must be on the same order: a credit note may point at an invoice or a
receipt, and an invoice only at the receipt it replaces. The
tables follow the database rules in `spree/AGENTS.md`. On the wire the
issuer is `{ type: 'store' | 'seller', id: 'sel_…' }`, following the rule
against Ruby class names in the API.

### Flows

**Upload.** `POST /api/v3/admin/orders/:id/invoices` and
`POST /api/v3/seller/orders/:id/invoices` take a signed blob, `kind`,
`number`, `issued_on`, optional `supplied_on`, `currency`, totals, an
optional `original_invoice_id` and `external_reference`. The issuer is
the order's (Decision 3); a seller can upload only to their own seller
orders. Commission invoices are uploaded by the operator to
`POST /api/v3/admin/seller_payouts/:id/invoices`. Uploads must be PDFs;
a credit note's totals must be zero or negative, and any other kind's total
zero or more.

**Integration pattern.** An external system listens to `order.paid`,
`refund.created` and `order.invoice_requested`, issues the document in
its own system and uploads it. The integration guide documents this.

**Invoice request.** `POST /api/v3/store/orders/:id/invoice_requests`
takes an optional company name, VAT number and billing address. Without
an address the request carries none, and the seller uses the order's. It is
refused outside the window, while a request is open, or when the order
already has an invoice of kind `invoice`. Creating an invoice of kind
`invoice` on the order fulfils the open request.

**Delivery.** When an invoice is added and `send_invoice_email` is on, a
new editable customer email, "Your invoice {{ invoice.number }}", sends
the PDF and sets `sent_at`. Commission invoices go to the seller's
billing email whatever `send_invoice_email` says, because the setting is
for stores whose own system emails buyers. The Store API lists a customer's invoices per order, and
each `download_url` is streamed through the API to whoever owns the order,
as return labels are (revised 2026-11-01 during implementation, rather
than expiring storage links). Buyers prepare a private upload under their
order (`POST /store/orders/:id/documents/upload`) before adding a
document, because a general store upload endpoint would let anyone with
the publishable key write to the merchant's storage. The storefront's
order page lists invoices in its Documents section with a "Request an
invoice" form.

### APIs, events, permissions

- **Admin API:** `/admin/invoices` (list with filters on kind, source,
  issuer, order and date; show; download; delete while unsent; CSV
  export), `/admin/orders/:id/invoices` (list, upload),
  `/admin/seller_payouts/:id/invoices` (list, upload),
  `/admin/orders/:id/invoice_requests` (list), `POST
  /admin/invoices/:id/resend`. Orders accept a `missing_invoice` filter.
- **Seller API:** the same, scoped to `current_seller`, including the
  seller's commission invoices (read only).
- **Store API:** invoices per order, download, create
  `invoice_requests`.
- **Events:** `invoice.created`, `invoice.sent`, `invoice.deleted`,
  `order.invoice_requested`. Payloads carry prefixed ids and the
  issuer's API type.
- **Permissions:** invoices, invoice requests and order documents are
  subjects of the existing `orders` permission, which sellers already hold,
  as returns are (revised 2026-11-01 during implementation: a new
  `invoices` scope would have needed a data backfill of every seller role,
  which has no precedent). Commission invoices on payouts follow the
  `payouts` permission.
- **MCP:** invoices readable through `search_resources` and
  `get_resource`.

### Dashboard and seller panel

- Order page: the Documents card (Decision 7), with upload, download,
  resend, delete while unsent, and any open request.
- Orders: a "Missing invoice" filter. Orders → Invoices: a store-wide
  list with CSV export, for accountants.
- Sellers (operator): a "Missing invoices" count per seller; the payout
  page lists and uploads commission invoices.
- Seller panel: the same Documents card, an Invoices list including
  commission invoices received, and the "Missing invoice" filter on their
  orders.
- Settings: the request window and the invoice email switch, beside the
  other order settings.

### What Enterprise adds

`ee/docs/plans/invoice-generation.md` designs generation on top of this
record: gapless number series per issuer, an EN 16931-complete snapshot,
pure-Ruby PDF rendering, automatic issuing when the order is paid,
receipts for consumers, credit notes on refunds, commission invoices per
payout, issuing on sellers' behalf under an invoicing agreement,
appearance settings, a master switch, and structured e-invoicing formats
(KSeF, Peppol, XRechnung, Factur-X). It needs these from open source:

- `Spree::Invoice` with `source: generated`, the `invoice.created` event,
  delivery and the Documents card, unchanged.
- `Spree::InvoiceRequest` and `order.invoice_requested`, so it can issue
  the invoice at once.
- The `missing_invoice` scope, to show what generation did not cover.
- `taxability_reason` on every tax line, including zero-amount rows.

### European legal requirements

| Requirement | Source | Where it is met |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invoice content and sequential numbering | VAT Directive Art. 226 | The issuing system: Enterprise generation, or the system that uploaded |
| Consumer invoice on request (Poland: three months) | Art. 221; Polish VAT Act art. 106b | Invoice requests, for every issuer (Decision 6) |
| Corrections reference the original | Art. 219 | Credit notes with `original_invoice`; no edits (Decision 4) |
| Integrity, authenticity, legibility for the storage period | Art. 233, 247 | Immutable record and file, kept through anonymization (Decisions 4, 9) |
| Structured e-invoices (Belgium, Poland, Croatia, Italy now; France, Germany 2026–2028; intra-EU B2B from July 2030 under ViDA) | Directive 2025/516 and national laws | The issuing system; Enterprise formats |

## Migration Path

1. **Shared private-file concern**, extracted from tax exemption
   certificates and seller requirement submissions.
2. **Records:** `spree_invoices`, `spree_invoice_requests`; uploads in the
   Admin and Seller APIs; events; the `invoices` permission.
3. **Dashboard and seller panel:** the Documents card (with
   `6.1-b2b-order-documents.md`), invoice lists, CSV export, the
   missing-invoice filter and seller counts, commission invoices on
   payouts.
4. **Store API and storefront:** invoices per order, download, invoice
   requests, the invoice email.
5. **Docs:** merchant guide, marketplace guide, and an integration guide
   for uploading from an external system on `order.paid`,
   `refund.created` and `order.invoice_requested`.
6. **Playwright specs** for uploading, delivery, requests and the
   missing-invoice view, in both panels.

Nothing to migrate.

## Constraints on Current Work

- **Every invoice, receipt and credit note is a `Spree::Invoice`**,
  uploaded or generated. Never an `OrderDocument` row or a loose
  attachment.
- **Never edit an invoice.** Corrections are credit notes. An upload may
  be deleted only while unsent.
- **Never treat an order number, payment number or `HasNumber` value as an
  invoice number.**
- **No numbering series, snapshot or PDF generation in core.** They are
  Enterprise; core's model must work without them.
- **Anonymization and seller deletion must not touch invoices or their
  files.** Seller legal identity stays soft-deleted with the seller,
  never hard-deleted.
- **Tax lines must keep writing `taxability_reason`, including on
  zero-amount rows.** Enterprise generation depends on it.

## Open Questions

None blocking 6.1. Deliberately deferred:

- **Store legal identity** (legal name, registration number, store tax
  identifiers) in open source. Only Enterprise generation reads it today.
  Unblocked by an open-source feature that shows the store's legal
  details, such as a storefront legal-notice page or an email footer,
  which EU consumer law and the German imprint rule ask shops for.
- **Customs commercial invoices** for exports. They are shipping
  documents, not VAT invoices, and belong with shipping labels and duties
  (`6.0-shipping-labels-and-deliveries.md`, `6.0-duties-and-custom-fees.md`).
