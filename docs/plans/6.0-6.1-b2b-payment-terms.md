# B2B Payment Terms, Deposits Schedule & Bank Transfer References

**Status:** Draft — design finalized 2026-08-30, nothing built
**Target:** Spree 6.0 (terms snapshot + fixes, riding the wholesale deposit work) / 6.1 (named terms, company assignment, bank transfer method, pay-balance)
**Depends on:** B2B Wholesale Shipping (6.0-b2b-wholesale-shipping.md — deposit collection, amended 2026-08-30), B2B Companies (6.0-b2b-companies-and-catalogs.md), Customer PO Numbers (6.0-b2b-customer-po-numbers.md), Document Numbers (6.0-document-numbers.md)
**Author:** Damian + Claude
**Last updated:** 2026-08-30

## Summary

Spree already understands order total, payment total and outstanding balance —
and multiple partial payments against one order work today (each capped at the
outstanding balance, `partially_paid` derived automatically, the dashboard
add-payment dialog is the "record the wire" UI). What's missing is the
commercial layer: *how much is due now*, *what the agreed terms are*, and a
*reconciliation-grade payment reference* for bank transfers. This plan adds
**deposit-style payment terms** (collect-first: 100% prepayment, N% deposit +
balance-before-X) as a frozen order snapshot resolved from layered sources, a
derived **payment schedule** ("Deposit due $8,000 → Deposit paid ✓ → Balance
$12,000 — due before shipping"), a real **`PaymentMethod::BankTransfer`** with
structured, stored, searchable **payment references** (no commerce platform
generates these natively — only one PSP does), and the storefront
**pay-the-balance** route. Ship-first terms (Net 7/30/60, credit accounts,
invoicing, due dates, dunning) remain **Enterprise roadmap item #1** — the
tier line is risk: collecting before shipping is OSS mechanics, extending
credit is the commercial instrument (decided 2026-08-30).

## Key Decisions (do not deviate without discussion)

- **One `payment_terms` snapshot on the order, layered sources**
  (2026-08-30, interactive review — amends the wholesale plan): the cart
  resolves `{kind, deposit_percentage, balance_due_label, source}` from
  **quote/order override → company terms → the freight method's deposit
  preferences → none (pay in full)** and the order freezes the copy at
  placement — agreed terms survive later changes to the company default,
  exactly like negotiated prices. The wholesale plan's method-level
  `deposit_percentage` is demoted from anchor to *default source*;
  `Cart#amount_due_at_checkout` now reads the snapshot. Same resolution
  shape as `capture_method`.
- **`Spree::PaymentTerm` is a store-scoped named preset** (6.1): `name`
  ("40% Deposit / 60% Before Shipping"), `kind` (`prepaid | deposit` — a
  `class_attribute` vocabulary Enterprise extends with `net`),
  `deposit_percentage`, `balance_due_label`. Companies reference one
  (`payment_term_id`, nullable); the order snapshot copies **values, never
  the reference** — a renamed preset must not rewrite history.
- **The balance-due moment is a label, not automation** (the merchant's own
  framing): `balance_due_label` ("Before shipping", "Before dispatch") is
  recorded and displayed; real due *dates* arrive with Enterprise net
  terms. OSS derives the **payment schedule** on read — due-now amount,
  deposit state (due / paid), outstanding balance, the label — exposed on
  store + admin serializers; nothing persisted, `Orders::UpdateStatuses`
  stays untouched (its `partially_paid` already tells the ledger truth;
  the schedule tells the commercial truth).
- **`PaymentMethod::BankTransfer` joins core** (6.1) beside
  `PaymentMethod::Check`: an offline method whose account details and
  instruction lines are public preferences (the `public_preference_keys`
  mechanism — labels configurable because banking fields differ by
  country), shown at checkout, in order mails and on the
  payment-instructions print.
- **Every payment gets a stored, generated `reference`** (6.1): unique,
  ransackable, created with the payment (structured creditor-reference
  style — RF/ISO 11649 check-digits over the payment's prefixed id — so a
  bank statement line matches exactly one payment). This is deliberately a
  **stored column**, never the derived `R1001-P1` number: derived numbers
  shift when a sibling is destroyed (the code's own idempotency warning)
  and their column is NULL on 6.0 rows — which is also why the derived
  number quietly broke search and the Stripe gateway's `find_by(number:)`
  lookup. **6.0 fixes (implemented 2026-08-30, riding the draft-order
  negotiation mechanics per the Wave 1 bundling)**: the misleading `number`
  ransack whitelist entry is removed, and the gateway lookup keys on a new
  `payment_prefixed_id` entry in `Spree::Payment::GatewayOptions#to_hash`
  (`payments.find_by_prefix_id`, stored-number lookup kept as the legacy
  fallback) — the prefixed id is the stable handle the idempotency key was
  already built on; 6.1's `reference` becomes the reconciliation identifier
  the dashboard searches alongside order and PO numbers.
- **Storefront pay-balance** (6.1): `POST /store/orders/:id/payments` /
  payment sessions on a placed order, owner-authorized, amount capped by
  the existing `max_amount` validation — resolves the wholesale plan's
  open question 1. The deprecated `Payments::Create` service is not
  touched (nothing calls it; removed in 7.0).
- **PO and terms stay separate** (merchant point 10, already our stance):
  a PO authorizes the purchase buyer-side; terms say when the merchant
  requires payment. No coupling anywhere.

## Design Details

- Snapshot storage: `payment_terms` jsonb on `spree_carts` +
  `spree_orders` (nil = pay in full — today's behavior); resolved by the
  cart, copied at completion with the purchase fields. Serializers expose
  the snapshot and the derived schedule
  (`{amount_due_now, deposit_amount, deposit_paid, outstanding_balance,
  balance_due_label}`).
- `Carts::Complete` validates payments against `amount_due_at_checkout`
  (already the wholesale design — unchanged, just re-sourced); the
  invoice-later `payment_pending:` path composes as before.
- Company: `payment_term_id` on `spree_companies` (admin form + serializer);
  Enterprise's quote flow writes the order-level override
  (`b2b-quotes.md` shows terms on the quotation and acceptance freezes
  them via placement — no quote-side fields).
- Bank reference surfaces: checkout confirmation + order mails ("Amount to
  pay now: $8,000 · Reference: RF71-…"), the payment-instructions print
  (`6.1-b2b-order-documents.md`), dashboard payment rows + the combined
  reference search (order number / PO number / payment reference).

## Migration Path

1. **6.0** (with wholesale phase 7): `payment_terms` jsonb + resolution
   (method source only), `amount_due_at_checkout` re-sourced, schedule
   value object + serializers; the `number` ransack/Stripe lookup fixes
   (fixes shipped early, 2026-08-30, with the negotiation mechanics).
2. **6.1**: `Spree::PaymentTerm` + company assignment + dashboard settings
   page; snapshot source layering completed; `PaymentMethod::BankTransfer`;
   payment `reference` column + generation + search; store pay-balance
   route + storefront balance UI.

## Constraints on Current Work

- **Terms are read from the order snapshot, never re-resolved** from
  company/method after placement.
- **Nothing durable keys on the derived payment number** (`R1001-P1` is
  display + gateway `order_id` only); reconciliation identifiers are the
  stored `reference`.
- **No net-terms/credit/due-date machinery in OSS** — `kind` values beyond
  `prepaid | deposit` and anything invoice-shaped belong to the Enterprise
  terms plan.
- New payment-collecting surfaces must respect `amount_due_at_checkout`
  vs. full total per the snapshot, and the existing `max_amount` cap.

## Open Questions

1. **Progress payments** (30/30/40 three-stage schedules) — the payment
   model supports arbitrary partials already; whether the *terms*
   vocabulary grows multi-stage schedules is deferred until real demand
   (Enterprise terms likely owns it).
