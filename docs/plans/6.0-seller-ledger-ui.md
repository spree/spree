# Seller Ledger UI — payment splits, transfers and payouts in both panels

**Status:** Implemented (2026-09-09) — backend, both SDKs, both panels, specs and E2E all in
**Target:** Spree 6.0
**Depends on:** `6.0-multi-vendor-marketplace.md` (Decision 5 — `PaymentSplit`; Decision 9 — the two-level ledger; Decision 10 — the seller branch; Phases 3, 3a, 3b — all shipped), `6.0-seller-order-management.md` (the seller order page this extends, and its money constraints), `6.0-admin-spa.md` (panel composition)
**Author:** Damian Legawiec
**Last updated:** 2026-09-09

## Summary

The marketplace's money model is complete on the backend — one customer payment recorded per child order as a `PaymentSplit`, a `SellerTransfer` credited to the seller when their order ships, a `SellerPayout` sweeping those transfers to the seller's bank, and Stripe Connect executing both — but nobody can see any of it. A seller's order page shows what the sale came to and what the marketplace charged them, and nothing about what was paid or what they earned; the seller panel has no earnings or payouts screens at all. The operator dashboard has the ledger endpoints in the SDK and consumes none of them: no transfers list, no payouts queue, no balance on a seller's page, and on an order that came out of a split checkout the payments card is empty, because the payment hangs off the group. This plan closes the marketplace plan's Phase 4 ledger items: the read-only balance, transfer and payout ledger in the seller panel, the payment share on a seller's order, and the operator's transfers list, payouts review queue with mark-paid, balance card and grouped-order payments.

## Key Decisions (do not deviate without discussion)

### 1. The seller sees their own share of the payment, never the payment (RESOLVED 2026-09-09)

Reaffirms the marketplace plan (Decision 10: "the seller sees its `payment_split`, never the group's `Payment`") and the seller order plan (`Seller::OrderSerializer` "does **not** gain `payments`"). The seller order serializer gains `payment_splits`, serialized by a `Seller::PaymentSplitSerializer` that carries the split's amounts and nothing about the payment behind it — no method, no gateway status, no source. A seller reconciling their statement needs "how much of my order was captured, refunded and is still refundable"; how the buyer paid the marketplace stays the marketplace's.

An order placed with a single seller does not split and has no `PaymentSplit` rows: the panel's payment card then shows only what the order already serializes — `payment_status`, `payment_total`, `amount_due` — and no per-payment rows. This is deliberate. Exposing the order's own `Payment` on that path would mean two shapes for one card, and would reopen the payments question the seller order plan closed. If a seller-facing payment row is ever wanted for ungrouped orders, it is a `Seller::PaymentSerializer` with the split's field list, never a subclass of the store or admin one.

### 2. The seller ledger gets its own read-only key, `seller_earnings` (RESOLVED 2026-09-09)

`Spree::SellerTransfer` and `Spree::SellerPayout` are subjects of the operator's `payouts` resource, which is closed to the seller audience by design (the catalog comment: a seller reads their own earnings through their own branch, scope-fetched, never through a key that could reach the whole ledger). The seller branch therefore gates its balance, transfer and payout endpoints on a new catalog resource:

```ruby
register_resource(:seller_earnings, group: :access, subjects: -> { [:seller_earnings] },
                                    write: false, audiences: %i[seller])
```

A symbol subject, exactly like `seller_profile` and for the same reason: the model classes belong to `payouts`, and claiming them twice would make `resource_for_subject` answer by registration order. Controllers declare `scoped_resource :seller_earnings` and override `authorize_resource!` to authorize the symbol (the `PoliciesController` pattern); which rows a seller may read is `current_seller` scope-fetching, never an ability rule.

Chosen over reusing `seller_profile` because a seller's owner must be able to give a packing teammate orders and fulfilments without showing them the money — settlement configuration is profile, but the ledger is the seller's books. A seller admin role is born with `grantable_keys(:seller)` at creation, so every seller created from here on holds the key; sellers are new in 6.0 and nothing older needs catching up, so there is no backfill (2026-09-09). A development database seeded before this key needs its seller role re-created or the key added by hand.

### 3. Operator ledger screens live under Sellers; the seller panel gets Earnings and Payouts (RESOLVED 2026-09-09)

Operator: `/sellers/transfers` and `/sellers/payouts` (+ `/sellers/payouts/:id`) as children of the existing Sellers nav entry, gated on `Subject.SellerTransfer` / `Subject.SellerPayout`. They are about sellers, the way returns and exchanges sit under Orders; a store selling only its own goods never sees the entry. The existing `settings/payouts` page (store defaults and provider choice) is untouched.

Seller panel: two top-level entries between Orders and Profile — **Earnings** (`/earnings`: the balance per currency, then the transfer list) and **Payouts** (`/payouts`, `/payouts/:id`: settlements, each itemizing the transfers it covers). Two entries rather than one because they answer different questions on different days: "what did I earn on that order" and "did last week's payout go out". Both gated on the `seller_earnings` subject.

### 4. Balance is a value object computed from the ledger, per currency, on both branches

`Spree::SellerBalance` (`ActiveModel::Model` + `ActiveModel::Attributes`, per the project's no-`Struct` rule) is one seller's position in one currency: `earned` (Σ completed transfers, reversals included), `paid` (Σ completed payouts), `balance` (earned − paid — what the marketplace still owes, the same figure `Seller#balance(currency)` already answers), and `pending` (Σ transfers still `pending`/`processing`/`unresolved` — money in flight with the provider, shown so a seller does not read a shipped order as unpaid). `Seller#balances` answers one per currency the seller has ever earned or been paid in, so a multi-currency marketplace never collapses two ledgers into one number.

Served by `GET /api/v3/seller/balances` and `GET /api/v3/admin/sellers/:id/balances`. Deliberately **not** a marketplace-wide "owed by seller" endpoint: the payouts list filtered to `owed` is the operator's queue, and the sweep is what turns a balance into something to act on.

### 5. A grouped order's payments card shows the group's payments, read-only, with this order's share (RESOLVED 2026-09-09)

On an order that came out of a split checkout, `order.payments` is empty and the operator's payments card renders "no payments" — which is wrong, not merely incomplete. The card fetches the order group (`GET /admin/order_groups/:id`, which already serializes `payments`) and the order's `payment_splits` (a new `expand`), and renders each payment with an extra column: this order's authorized / captured / refunded share. Capture and void are **not** offered on a grouped child: those act on the group's payment, i.e. on every sibling's money, and belong on a surface that says so. `Admin::OrderSerializer#payments` keeps its meaning (the order's own rows), so the nested `orders/:id/payments` controller and everything reading `order.payments` are unchanged.

### 6. Read and write stay where they are

Nothing here creates, edits or moves money. Transfers and payouts are written by fulfilment and the sweep; the one write in scope is the existing `PATCH /admin/seller_payouts/:id/complete` (the built-in provider's "the bank transfer went out"), which the operator's payouts pages now expose with a reference field. The seller branch is read-only end to end — a seller sees `unresolved` and `failed` rows with their status and the marketplace's contact is how they follow up.

## Design Details

### Backend

**Core**

- `seller_earnings` catalog resource.
- `Spree::SellerBalance` — `spree/core/app/models/spree/seller_balance.rb`. Attributes `seller`, `currency`, `earned`, `paid`, `balance`, `pending` (decimals), `display_*` via `Spree::Money`. `SellerBalance.for(seller, currency)` computes from `seller.seller_transfers` / `seller.seller_payouts` in four grouped sums; `Seller#balances` maps it over `currencies = transfers.distinct.pluck(:currency) | payouts.distinct.pluck(:currency)`. `Seller#balance(currency)` stays as the canonical single figure (the payout sweep reads it).
- `Spree::SellerTransfer.whitelisted_ransackable_attributes` += `seller_id order_id payout_id`; `Spree::SellerPayout.whitelisted_ransackable_attributes` += `seller_id`. Without these a `q[order_id_eq]` filter silently matches nothing (the lesson recorded in the marketplace plan's Phase 4 note). The base controller already decodes prefixed ids in `*_id_eq` predicates.
- `Spree::PaymentSplit`: `claimed_amount` joins `money_methods` so every amount has a `display_` twin; `net_captured_amount` and `refundable_amount` get display twins the same way.

**Seller API** (`/api/v3/seller`, all read-only, all `scoped_resource :seller_earnings` with the `PoliciesController`-style authorization override, all rooted in `current_seller`):

- `GET /balances` — `Seller::BalancesController < Seller::BaseController` (a computed collection, no model; renders `current_seller.balances`).
- `GET /transfers`, `GET /transfers/:id` — `Seller::TransfersController < Seller::ResourceController`, `seller_association :seller_transfers`, `collection_includes [:order, :payout]`.
- `GET /payouts`, `GET /payouts/:id` — `Seller::PayoutsController`, `seller_association :seller_payouts`, with the same page-wide `transfer_counts` the admin controller builds.
- Serializers, all from `V3::BaseSerializer` (never a subclass of the admin ones — the seller order plan's constraint):
  - `Seller::TransferSerializer`: `id kind status currency amount display_amount provider reference order_id order_number payout_id reversed_from_id created_at updated_at`. No `metadata` (provider internals), no `refund_id` (refunds are not a seller resource), no `seller_id`/`seller_name` (there is one seller in play).
  - `Seller::PayoutSerializer`: `id status currency amount display_amount provider reference period_start period_end transfers_count created_at updated_at`.
  - `Seller::BalanceSerializer`: `currency earned paid balance pending` + `display_` twins. `id` is `nil` — the base serializer renders `id` only for objects answering `prefixed_id`; the SDK type marks it absent.
  - `Seller::PaymentSplitSerializer`: `id currency authorized_amount captured_amount refunded_amount claimed_amount net_captured_amount refundable_amount` + `display_` twins, `created_at`. `Seller::OrderSerializer` gains `many :payment_splits`, always rendered (an order page fetches one order; a list never embeds them because the seller orders index uses the same serializer — so the relation is gated `if: proc { expand?('payment_splits') }`, and the panel's order query asks for it).
- Routes: `resources :balances, only: [:index]`, `resources :transfers, only: [:index, :show]`, `resources :payouts, only: [:index, :show]` in the seller block, next to `orders`.

**Admin API**:

- `Admin::PaymentSplitSerializer`: the seller field list plus `payment_id`, `payment_number`, `order_id`, `order_number`, `updated_at`. Registered as `admin_payment_split_serializer`.
- `Admin::OrderSerializer` gains `many :payment_splits, if: expand?('payment_splits')`; `Admin::PaymentSerializer` gains the same, so a group's payment can itemize its shares.
- `GET /admin/sellers/:id/balances` — `Admin::Sellers::BalancesController`, `scoped_resource :payouts`, seller fetched through `current_store.sellers`. `Admin::SellerBalanceSerializer` adds `seller_id` to the seller shape.
- `Admin::OrdersController#show` `expand` allowlist admits `payment_splits`; `Admin::OrderGroupsController#show` keeps serializing `payments` (already does).

**Permissions**: the `seller_earnings` resource (Decision 2). Seller endpoints authorize `:seller_earnings`; admin balances sit under `payouts`; payment splits ride on `orders` (the order expand) and `payments` (`PaymentSplit` is already a `payments` subject). The seller panel gates the Earnings and Payouts entries and the order page's earnings card on the `seller_earnings` subject; the payment card rides on the order itself.

**OpenAPI**: integration specs under `spree/api/spec/integration/spree/api/v3/seller/{balances,transfers,payouts}_spec.rb` and `admin/sellers/balances_spec.rb`; `swaggerize` regenerates `seller.yaml` and `admin.yaml`. Typelizer emits `Balance`, `Transfer`, `Payout`, `PaymentSplit` into `@spree/seller-sdk` and `PaymentSplit`, `SellerBalance` into `@spree/admin-sdk`.

### SDKs

- `@spree/seller-sdk`: `balances.list()`, `transfers.list(params)` / `get(id)`, `payouts.list(params)` / `get(id)`; `orders.get` accepts `expand`.
- `@spree/admin-sdk`: `sellers.balances(id)`, `orderGroups.get(id)` (the endpoint exists, the client never wrapped it), `orders.get` already takes `expand`.

### Seller panel (`@spree/seller-dashboard`)

```
nav/default.ts                          → earnings (130), payouts (140), subject 'seller_earnings'
routes/_authenticated/$sellerId/earnings/index.tsx
routes/_authenticated/$sellerId/payouts/index.tsx
routes/_authenticated/$sellerId/payouts/$payoutId.tsx
pages/earnings.tsx                      → BalanceSummary + ResourceTable<Transfer> ('seller-transfers')
pages/payouts.tsx                       → ResourceTable<Payout> ('seller-payouts')
pages/payout.tsx                        → header (status, amount, reference, period) + the transfers it settled
components/finance/balance-summary.tsx  → one card per currency: balance owed, pending; empty state before the first sale
components/orders/order-payment-card.tsx   → payment status, paid, due; split rows when the order is grouped
components/orders/order-earnings-card.tsx  → this order's transfers: kind, status, amount, payout link; nothing until fulfilled
tables/seller-transfers.tsx, tables/seller-payouts.tsx
hooks/use-balances.ts, use-transfers.ts, use-payouts.ts
```

The order page mounts the payment card after the summary card and the earnings card after it, both in the main column. The transfers table filters by `status`, `kind` and `created_at`; the payouts table by `status` and `created_at`. Rows open the payout page; a transfer row links to its order. `unresolved` joins `statusToneMap` (warning) in `@spree/dashboard-ui`, since both panels render it.

### Operator dashboard (`@spree/dashboard`)

```
nav/default.ts                          → sellers gains children: sellers.transfers, sellers.payouts
routes/_authenticated/$storeId/sellers/transfers.tsx     → ResourceTable<SellerTransfer>
routes/_authenticated/$storeId/sellers/payouts/index.tsx → ResourceTable<SellerPayout>, row action "Mark as paid"
routes/_authenticated/$storeId/sellers/payouts/$payoutId.tsx → header + complete + its transfers
tables/seller-transfers.tsx             → filters: seller (resource), status, kind, provider, created_at; links to order/seller/payout
tables/seller-payouts.tsx               → filters: seller (resource), status, provider, created_at; default quick filter "owed"
components/spree/sellers/seller-balance-card.tsx       → sidebar of the seller page, per currency, links to both lists filtered by seller
components/spree/sellers/payout-complete-dialog.tsx    → reference input; shown only for owed payouts
components/spree/orders/order-payments-card.tsx        → grouped branch: group payments + this order's share, no actions
components/spree/orders/order-seller-transfers-card.tsx → the earning and its reversals for a seller's order
hooks/use-seller-ledger.ts              → useSellerBalances, usePayout, useCompletePayout, useOrderGroup
```

"Mark as paid" confirms through the dialog (the reference is the confirmation — no second confirm on top). It is offered for `pending`, `processing` and `unresolved` payouts, matching `SellerPayouts::Complete`'s compare-and-swap. Every new key lands in all six dashboard locales; the seller panel ships `en.json` only, as today.

### E2E

`packages/dashboard/e2e/seller-ledger.spec.ts`: seed a seller with a completed transfer and an owed payout through the API fixtures, open the payouts queue, mark it paid with a reference, assert the status badge and the reference on the detail page. Seller-panel coverage stays at controller + integration specs until that package has an e2e stack.

## Migration Path

No schema change; everything was additive. Shipped in this order:

1. **Core + Admin API.** `Spree::SellerBalance`, `Seller#balances`, the `seller_earnings` catalog resource, Ransack allowlist additions (`seller_id` on both ledger models, plus `order_id`/`payout_id` on transfers), `PaymentSplit` display methods for `claimed_amount`, `net_captured_amount` and `refundable_amount`, the admin payment-split and seller-balance serializers, `payment_splits` expands on the admin order and payment serializers, and `GET /admin/sellers/:id/balances`.
2. **Seller API.** `BalancesController`, `TransfersController`, `PayoutsController`, four serializers, routes, and `payment_splits` on the seller order serializer behind an expand.
3. **SDKs.** Typelizer regeneration, `balances`/`transfers`/`payouts` on the seller client with `orders.get` taking an expand, `sellers.balances` and a new `orderGroups` resource on the admin client.
4. **Seller panel.** The payment and earnings cards on the order page, then Earnings, then Payouts and the payout detail page.
5. **Operator dashboard.** The grouped-payments branch on the payments card, the seller-transfers card on the order page, the balance card on the seller page, the two ledger lists, the payout page, the mark-as-paid dialog, nav children, and locale keys in all six languages.

`ReadRow` was extracted out of the seller panel's profile page into `components/read-row.tsx` rather than copied a third time.

**Sample data.** `spree:sellers:sample_data` gained a fund ledger, so the new screens have something to render in a fresh development environment: five sales, a completed payout carrying its bank reference, one still owed for the mark-as-paid flow, an earning not yet swept into either, and a refund reversal. The reversal is deliberately left unsettled — a refund after its sale was already paid out belongs to the next period rather than to a closed settlement, which is the behaviour the ledger models. Written directly rather than through fulfilment and the sweep: reaching those would mean placing and shipping real orders, and the screens read the rows either way. Idempotent, and development/test only like the rest of that task.

## Constraints on Current Work

- **A seller serializer for money carries amounts, never provenance.** `Seller::PaymentSplitSerializer` must not grow `payment_id`, a method name, a gateway status or a source; `Seller::TransferSerializer` must not grow `metadata` or `refund_id`. Anything that identifies the marketplace's payment or the buyer's instrument is the admin serializer's.
- **Seller ledger endpoints authorize `:seller_earnings`** and root in `current_seller`. Do not add `payouts` or `payments` to the seller audience to make `authorize!` on the model class pass — that is the leak the catalog comment warns about.
- **A seller role holds the keys it was created with.** Adding a seller-audience key to the catalog reaches new roles only; that is acceptable while sellers are new in 6.0, and a key added after 6.0 ships is a different conversation.
- **Nothing on either panel offers capture, void or refund on a grouped child's payment** until a surface exists that names every sibling the action touches.
- **Filters go through the allowlist.** A new `q[<column>_eq]` on the ledger needs the column in `whitelisted_ransackable_attributes`; an association name alone does not make its foreign key filterable.
- **`Seller#balance(currency)` stays the single figure the sweep reads.** `SellerBalance` presents it; it must not compute a different number.
