These settings decide where customers shop and what stands between them and a finished order: whether they must sign in, whether they can buy as a guest, and what an address form asks for.

To configure them, navigate to **Settings → Storefront & checkout**, under the **Store** group.

## Storefront

Where your storefront lives and who can see it before signing in. This is the difference between an open retail shop and a trade store where prices are only shown to approved customers.

- **Storefront URL** - The address customers shop at. Emails and the dashboard link to it. Click **Connect**, or **Change** once one is set, to open the connect panel: it saves the URL and allows your storefront to call the Store API from that address.
- **Storefront access** - The store-wide default for anonymous visitors:
  - **Public** - Anyone can browse and see prices.
  - **Prices hidden** - Visitors must sign in to see prices. Guests must sign in to check out.
  - **Login required** - Visitors must sign in to browse at all.

<Note>Storefront access and guest checkout are defaults that a sales channel can override. If you change one here and a storefront does not behave as expected, check that channel's own settings rather than changing this again.</Note>

The two restricted options do different things, and the difference matters:

- **Prices hidden** is how most wholesale and trade stores work. A visitor can browse the range and see what you sell, but not what it costs, until you have approved their account.
- **Login required** goes further and closes the catalogue as well. A visitor sees nothing at all until they sign in, so choose it only when the range itself is confidential.

## Checkout

How checkout behaves for every order.

- **Guest checkout** - Whether shoppers can check out without creating an account.
- **Always show confirmation step** - Give shoppers a final review step before placing the order, even when no payment method requires one.

## Addresses

What customers are asked for when entering an address. Every extra field is one more thing between a shopper and a completed order, so only ask for what you will actually use.

- **Company field** - Show a company field on address forms during checkout. Worth turning on if you sell to businesses.
- **Require phone number** - Make a phone number mandatory on customer addresses. Some carriers need one for delivery.

## Saving Changes

Click **Save** at the top of the page. The button is greyed out until something has changed. The storefront URL is not part of this save: the connect panel saves it on its own.
