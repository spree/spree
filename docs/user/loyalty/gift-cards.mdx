title: "Gift Cards"
description: "Learn how to issue gift cards, follow their balances and redeem them on orders in Spree Commerce."
---

A gift card is money paid to you up front, by a customer buying a present for someone, or given away by you as a goodwill gesture or a prize. Whoever holds it spends the value in your shop later.

Each card holds an amount in one currency. When a customer enters the code at checkout, the card pays what it can of the order, and anything left stays on the card for their next visit. Every card keeps a running record of how much has been spent and how much remains, so you always know what you still owe.

Gift cards are not the same as store credit. Store credit is a balance you give one particular customer, from their profile or when you refund a return, and it is covered on the [<u>Store Credits List</u>](/user/loyalty/store-credits-list) page.

With a handful of cards a year, the list is a simple record of who holds what. Once you sell cards over the counter or give them out in batches for a campaign, it becomes the place you check before you honour a code and the record your accountant asks for at year end.

Gift cards sit with store credit under Loyalty rather than under Promotions. To manage gift cards, navigate to **Loyalty → Gift Cards** in the admin dashboard.

## Reviewing Your Gift Cards

The list shows one row for every card you have issued, newest first.

![The Gift Cards list with one active card](/docs/images/user/loyalty/gift-cards/1-gift-cards-overview.png)

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Code** | What the customer types at checkout. When the card belongs to a customer, their email address sits under the code. |
| **Status** | Whether the card can still be spent. See the table below. |
| **Amount** | What the card was worth when you issued it. |
| **Used** | How much has been spent on orders so far. |
| **Remaining** | What the card can still pay for. This is what you still owe on it. |
| **Expires** | The last date the card is valid, or a dash when it never expires. |

Type a code into the search box to check a card a customer has brought to you. The **Status** filter answers "which cards still have money on them", and **Add filter** narrows the list by currency, batch, customer, who issued the card, expiry date, or when the card was created or last changed.

A card moves through these statuses as it is spent:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Nothing has been spent yet. The full amount is available. |
| **Partially redeemed** | Some of the value has been spent and the rest is still available. |
| **Redeemed** | The whole amount has been spent. The card is kept on the list as a record, but nothing is left on it. |

## How to Issue a Gift Card

Click **New gift card** in the top-right corner. A panel opens over the list with these fields:

- **Quantity** - How many cards to issue. Leave it at 1 for a single card.
- **Code** - The code the customer will type. Leave it blank and a code of sixteen letters and numbers is made for you. Type your own when you want something memorable for a campaign.
- **Amount** - What the card is worth. The card cannot be created without an amount above zero.
- **Currency** - The currency the card's value is held in.
- **Expires** - Set a date if your gift card terms limit how long a card is valid. Leave it on **No expiry** otherwise.
- **Customer (optional)** - The customer the card belongs to. Start typing their email address and pick them from the list.

Fill in the panel and click **Create gift card**. The panel closes, a message confirms the card was created, and the new card is at the top of the list with its full amount under **Remaining**.

![The new gift card at the top of the list, attached to a customer](/docs/images/user/loyalty/gift-cards/2-new-gift-card.png)

Creating the card does not send it to anyone, so pass the code on to the customer yourself, by email, on a printed card or at the till. The code is all they need to spend it.

## How to Check a Card's Balance

Click a card's code in the list, or open the row menu and choose **Edit**. A panel opens with the card's code as its title. Its **Usage** section shows what the card was worth, how much has been spent, what remains and which member of staff issued it.

![A gift card's panel showing its usage](/docs/images/user/loyalty/gift-cards/3-gift-card-detail.png)

Once a card is fully spent, every box in the panel is greyed out and the panel is there to read.

## How Customers Redeem a Card at Checkout

Customers spend a card in your shop's checkout. They type the code into the **Gift card or discount code** box in the order summary and click **Apply**. The summary then shows a gift card line taking the card's value off the order, and the amount still to pay underneath.

When the card covers the whole order, the payment section reads *No payment required* and the customer clicks **Place Order**. When the card covers only part of it, the customer picks a payment method for the rest, such as **Check**, and places the order. The order page then lists both payments, one for the card's share and one for the rest.

![The checkout summary with a gift card paying part of the order and the rest due](/docs/images/user/loyalty/gift-cards/4-checkout-summary.png)

A card that is not attached to a customer can be spent by anyone who has the code, including a shopper checking out as a guest. A card with money left after an order keeps that balance, and the customer can use the same code again on a later order until nothing remains.

On the order page in the admin dashboard, the **Discounts & Credit** card shows which gift card paid for the order and how much it covered.

## How to Apply a Gift Card to a Draft Order

When your team builds an order on a customer's behalf, you can take a gift card as part of the payment.

1. Open the draft order. In the **Discounts & Credit** card, click **Apply** next to **Gift card**.
2. Type the card's code and click **Add**.
3. The card's code and the amount it covers appear in the **Gift card** row, and the order's **Outstanding balance** drops by the same amount.

If the card does not cover the whole order, add a payment for the rest from the **Payments** card before you complete the order. If you add more items after applying the card, the card stays at the amount it covered when you applied it, and the extra is added to the outstanding balance.

To take a card off a draft order, click **Remove** in the **Gift card** row and confirm with **Remove**. The order's outstanding balance goes back up, and the amount returns to the card's remaining balance.

## How to Delete a Gift Card

Open the row menu on the card and choose **Delete**, then confirm with **Delete**. The card disappears from the list.

<Warning>Deleting a card erases it from your store's records rather than keeping it as a cancelled card. If the card still has a balance, that balance goes with it, so check **Remaining** first and settle up with the customer before you delete.</Warning>
