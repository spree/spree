title: "Issue a Gift Card"

A gift card is a prepaid balance your customer spends later. People buy them as presents, and shops hand them out as a thank-you or to settle a complaint without a cash refund.

Each card has a code and a balance. Your customer types the code at checkout, and the card pays what it can of the order. Clicking **Create gift card** in the dashboard makes the card ready to use at once.

Store credit is the neighbour to know about. It sits on one customer's account rather than on a code, and you issue it from that customer's page. See [<u>Issue Store Credits</u>](/user/loyalty/store-credits).

Keep your own note of why each card was given, whether it was sold, handed out as goodwill or offered in place of a refund.

To issue gift cards, navigate to **Loyalty → Gift Cards** in the admin dashboard.

## How to Issue a Gift Card

Click **New gift card** in the top-right corner of the Gift Cards list. A panel opens over the list with these fields:

![The New gift card panel filled in for one $50.00 card attached to a customer](/docs/images/user/loyalty/issue-gift-card/1-the-new-gift-card-panel-filled-in-for.png)

- **Quantity** - How many cards to issue. Leave it at 1 for a single card.
- **Code** - What the customer types at checkout. Leave it blank and a 16-character code of letters and numbers is made for you.
- **Amount** - The value of the card. It has to be more than zero, and the panel stays open with a message under **Amount** until it is.
- **Currency** - The currency the balance is held in. Choose it with care, because the box is locked once the card is issued.
- **Expires** - Shows **No expiry** until you pick a date from the calendar it opens.
- **Customer (optional)** - The customer the card is for. Start typing their email to find them. Once issued, their email shows under the card's code in the list, so you can see at a glance who holds it.

Fill in the panel and click **Create gift card**. The panel closes, a *Gift card created* message appears, and the new card sits at the top of the list as **Active**, with its full amount under **Remaining** and nothing under **Used**.

![The Gift Cards list after issuing a card, with the new card at the top and the customer's email under its code](/docs/images/user/loyalty/issue-gift-card/2-the-gift-cards-list-after-issuing-a.png)

<Note>Issuing a card does not send it to anyone. Copy the code from the list and pass it on to the customer yourself.</Note>

### When Quantity is more than one

Type a number above 1 in **Quantity** and the panel changes to suit a batch of cards. **Code** becomes **Prefix**, the **Customer (optional)** field goes away, and the button counts the cards, for example **Create 3 gift cards**.

## How to Follow a Card Once It Is Spent

Your customer types the code into the gift card or discount code box at checkout and clicks **Apply**. The order summary then shows the gift card as a line taken off the total, with the amount still to pay underneath. When the card covers the whole order, the payment step reads *No payment required*. When it covers part of it, the customer pays the rest another way.

Back on the Gift Cards list, each card shows how much of it has gone:

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Amount** | What the card was worth when you issued it. |
| **Used** | How much the customer has spent from it so far. |
| **Remaining** | What is left to spend. |
| **Expires** | A dash means the card was issued with **No expiry**. |

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Issued, with its full amount still to spend. |
| **Redeemed** | The whole amount has been spent. **Remaining** reads $0.00. |
