Sometimes you want to make something right for a customer without sending money back to their card: a parcel that arrived late, a damaged item they decided to keep, a price drop the week after they bought. Store credit is how you do that.

Store credit is money your store owes one particular customer, held on their account. You issue it from the customer's own page, and the total your store owes across every customer is added up in the [<u>Store Credits List</u>](/user/loyalty/store-credits-list). Issuing credit adds a line to that customer's store credit and raises the balance shown at the top of their page, and it changes nothing else: their order count and total spent stay as they were, and no order, payment or stock is touched.

A store credit belongs to one customer's account. If you want to give someone a code that is typed in at checkout instead, issue a gift card, see [<u>Gift Cards</u>](/user/loyalty/gift-cards).

To issue store credit, navigate to **Customers** in the admin dashboard and open the customer.

## Reviewing a Customer's Store Credit

Every customer page has a **Store credit** card, and the summary across the top of the page carries a **Store credit** figure beside **Total spent** and **Orders**. A customer who has never had credit shows "No store credits issued" on the card. Once credit exists, the card title carries a count and lists one line per credit:

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Amount** | How much was given in this credit. |
| **Used** | How much of it the customer has already spent. |
| **Remaining** | What is still left to spend. |
| **Memo** | The reason recorded when the credit was given. |

Each line has its own menu at the end of the row, which is where you delete a credit. To see every credit across all your customers, and what your store owes in total, use the [<u>Store Credits List</u>](/user/loyalty/store-credits-list) under **Loyalty**.

## How to Issue Store Credit

Click **Issue store credit** on the customer's **Store credit** card. A small form opens over the page with three fields:

- **Amount** - How much credit to give. The form does not go through while it is blank.
- **Currency** - The currency the credit is held in.
- **Memo** - Why you are giving it, such as "Goodwill for a late delivery" or the complaint it settles. Anyone who looks at this balance later, including a colleague answering the customer, will want to know where the money came from.

Fill in the form and click **Issue store credit**. The form closes, and without reloading the page the new line appears on the **Store credit** card and the **Store credit** figure at the top goes up by the amount you gave. The form and its button also fit a phone screen, so you can issue credit away from your desk.

![The Issue store credit form filled in with an amount of 25 US dollars and a memo](/docs/images/user/loyalty/store-credits/1-issue-store-credit.png)

The credit is recorded as issued by hand, together with the staff member who issued it, and both show on the credit's detail in the [<u>Store Credits List</u>](/user/loyalty/store-credits-list).

Write a memo every time. A balance nobody can explain is hard to defend when the customer, or your accountant, asks about it.

## How to Refund a Return as Store Credit

The second way a customer gets store credit is through a return. When you refund a return, you choose whether the money goes back to the original payment or onto the customer's account as store credit.

1. Open the order, and once the returned items have been received, open the return's menu and choose **Refund**.
2. Check the **Amount**, which starts at the return's refund total.
3. Set **Refund to** to **Store credit**, then click **Refund**.
4. The return is marked **Refunded**. On the customer's page a new line appears on the **Store credit** card, with a memo naming the return, and in the [<u>Store Credits List</u>](/user/loyalty/store-credits-list) its origin reads **Return**.

The steps before the refund, creating, approving and receiving the return, are covered in [<u>Returns</u>](/user/orders/returns).

## How to Delete Store Credit

If a credit was given by mistake, for the wrong amount or to the wrong customer, delete it and issue a fresh one.

1. On the customer's **Store credit** card, open the menu at the end of the credit's line and choose **Delete**.
2. A box asks you to confirm. Click **Delete**.
3. The line disappears from the card, and the **Store credit** figure at the top of the page drops by the credit's amount. When it was the customer's only credit, the card goes back to "No store credits issued".

![The confirmation box asking whether to delete a store credit](/docs/images/user/loyalty/store-credits/2-confirm-delete.png)

<Warning>Deleting a credit takes its whole amount off the customer's balance, and the line is gone from both their page and the Store Credits List. Make sure the customer has not been promised that money before you delete it.</Warning>
