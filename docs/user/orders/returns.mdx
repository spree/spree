title: "Returns"
description: "Learn how to take goods back from a customer and refund them in Spree Commerce."

A return is the process by which a customer sends faulty or unwanted goods back to you for a refund. It follows the goods from the moment the customer asks to send something back, through approving the request and counting in what arrives, to the refund. Units you count in as resellable go back into stock at the warehouse you chose, and the refund goes either to the customer's original payment or onto their store credit.

If the customer wants a different size, color or product instead of their money, start an [<u>exchange</u>](/user/orders/exchanges). If a delivery went wrong, such as an item that arrived damaged or never arrived, open a [<u>claim</u>](/user/orders/claims).

With a handful of orders a month you could track returns by email. Once several people handle returns, or goods come back to more than one warehouse, a return record tells everyone what was asked for, what actually arrived and what has been paid back.

Every return belongs to an order and is worked on that order's **Returns** card. Clicking a return's number in the list opens its order. The **Returns** item in the sidebar carries a count of returns not yet refunded, and the **Open returns** tile on Home shows the same queue. To manage returns, navigate to **Orders → Returns** in the admin dashboard.

## Reviewing Returns

The Returns list shows every return in the store, whichever order it belongs to.

![The Returns list with one requested return](/docs/images/user/orders/returns/1-returns-overview.png)

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Number** | The return's own reference. Click it to open the order the return belongs to. |
| **Status** | How far the return has got. See the table under [How to Approve and Receive a Return](#how-to-approve-and-receive-a-return). |
| **Orders** | The order the goods came from. |
| **Refund total** | The value of the goods on the return. Once you have counted the goods in, it shows the value of what actually arrived. |
| **Created at** | When the return was opened. |

Use the **Status** filter to see what needs you next: **Requested** returns are waiting for your approval, **Approved** ones are waiting for the goods, and **Received** ones are waiting for a refund. The search box finds a return by its number, and **Add filter** narrows the list by number or by the date a return was created or last updated.

## How to Create a Return

Open the order the goods came from and click **New return** on its **Returns** card.

![The New return dialog, with the quantity at 0 and the Reason, Return to and Note fields](/docs/images/user/orders/returns/2-new-return.png)

The dialog lists each item that was fulfilled on the order, with a quantity box beside it.

- **Quantity** - How many of that item the customer is sending back. Every box starts at 0, so nothing goes on the return unless you put it there, and you cannot enter more than were sent.
- **Reason** - Why the customer is sending it back. The choices come from **Settings → Returns & reasons**, where you can add your own, see [<u>Returns & Reasons</u>](/user/settings/returns-and-reasons).
- **Return to** - The stock location the goods are coming back to. Leave it on **Default returns location** or pick one of your warehouses.
- **Note** - Anything your team should know about this return, such as what the customer told you about the item's condition.

Enter the quantity for each item coming back, choose a **Reason**, and click **New return**. The button stays disabled until at least one quantity is above 0.

The order's **Returns** card then shows the new return as **Requested**, with the items, the warehouse it is returning to and its **Refund total**.

![The order's Returns card with the new return listed as Requested](/docs/images/user/orders/returns/3-return-created.png)

If you try to return more units than are left to return on that order, the return is not created and a message at the bottom of the screen tells you how many can still be returned.

## How to Approve and Receive a Return

A return moves through five statuses. The **Actions** button on the return offers only the next steps that make sense for its status.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Requested** | The customer has asked to send goods back. Nothing has moved yet. **Actions** offers **Approve** and **Cancel**. |
| **Approved** | You have agreed to take the goods back and are waiting for them. **Actions** offers **Receive items** and **Cancel**. |
| **Received** | The goods have been counted in. Resellable units are back in stock. **Actions** offers **Refund** only. |
| **Refunded** | The money has gone back to the customer. The return is finished and has no **Actions** button. |
| **Canceled** | The return was called off before any money moved. The units can go on a new return. |

1. On the order's **Returns** card, click **Actions** on the return and choose **Approve**. The status changes to **Approved** straight away.
2. When the parcel arrives, click **Actions** and choose **Receive items**. The **Receive returned items** dialog opens with the **Received** box filled in with the full quantity on the return.
3. Change **Received** to the number of units that actually arrived. Leave **Resellable** switched on if they can go back on sale. The switch covers every unit on that line.
4. Click **Receive items**. The return reads **Received**, the line shows how many arrived against how many were expected (for example **2 of 3 received**), and the **Refund total** drops to the value of what arrived. The resellable units are added back to stock at the return's warehouse.

Count what is in front of you rather than what the customer said they sent. The figure you enter is what the refund amount is worked out from.

![A return on the order page after receiving 1 of 1 units](/docs/images/user/orders/returns/4-return-received.png)

### Sending the Customer a Return Label

A return starts with **No return label yet**, and **Buy return label** on the return buys a prepaid one for the customer to put on the parcel.

The button is there while the return is **Requested** or **Approved**, and disappears once the goods have been received, since a label is no use after the parcel has arrived.

<Warning>Buying a label charges your carrier account as soon as the label is issued, so it needs a carrier account connected to your store. A label bought and then not used can be refunded from the return.</Warning>

A label bought outside Spree can be recorded against the return instead, so the return still carries the tracking whoever bought it.

## How to Refund a Return

Once a return is **Received**, click **Actions** and choose **Refund**. The **Refund return** dialog opens.

- **Amount** - What the customer gets back. It is filled in with the price of the units you received, without the delivery charge, and you can change it before you refund.
- **Refund to** - Where the money goes. **Original payment** sends it back to the card or method the customer paid with. **Store credit** adds it to the customer's store credit balance, which you can see on their customer page, so they can spend it in your store.

Check both, then click **Refund**. The dialog closes, the return reads **Refunded** and its **Actions** button disappears. After a refund to the original payment, the order's payment status changes to **Partially Refunded** when only part of the order was paid back, and the customer receives an email saying their return has been refunded, with the total amount refunded. The return also drops out of the count on the **Returns** sidebar item, as it has now been settled.

![The Refund return dialog with the amount for the received units and Refund to set to Original payment](/docs/images/user/orders/returns/5-refund-return.png)

<Warning>A refund to the original payment is sent to the customer's payment provider as soon as you click **Refund**, with no second confirmation. Check the **Amount** and **Refund to** before you click.</Warning>
