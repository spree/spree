---
title: "Claims"
description: "Learn how to record a delivery that went wrong and settle it with a refund or a replacement in Spree Commerce."
---

A claim is the process by which a customer reports that their order arrived damaged, incomplete or wrong, and you settle it with a refund, a replacement, or both. You open the claim on the order, record how many units were affected and why, then resolve it or turn it down.

What sets a claim apart is that nothing comes back. The goods stay with the customer, so a refund on a claim does not return anything to your stock, and a replacement is a new fulfillment on the order for you to send out. Use a [<u>return</u>](/user/orders/returns) when the customer is sending goods back, and an [<u>exchange</u>](/user/orders/exchanges) when they are swapping one item for another.

Claims are worth recording even when you settle them on the spot, because the record is what answers a customer, or a carrier, who asks months later what happened to a parcel.

To manage claims, navigate to **Orders → Claims** in the admin dashboard.

## Reviewing Claims

The Claims list shows every claim across your orders, one row per claim.

![The Claims list before any claim exists](/docs/images/user/orders/claims/1-claims-overview.png)

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Number** | The claim's own reference, starting with CLM. Clicking it opens the order the claim belongs to, which is where you work on it. |
| **Status** | How far the claim has got. See the table under [How to Resolve a Claim](#how-to-resolve-a-claim). |
| **Orders** | The order the claim was raised against. |
| **Created at** | When the claim was opened. |

Use the **Status** filter to see what still needs a decision (**Open** and **Approved**) apart from what is settled (**Resolved** and **Denied**). The search box finds a claim by its number, and **Add filter** narrows the list by number or by the date a claim was created or last updated.

## How to Open a Claim

Claims are opened from the order, not from the Claims list. Open the order and find the **Claims** card.

<Note>The claim form lists only the units you have already sent, so mark the order as fulfilled before you open a claim.</Note>

Click **New claim**. The form has the following fields:

- **Quantity** (one box per item sent) - How many units of that item went wrong. Each box starts at 0 and goes up to the number of units you sent.
- **Refund amount** - Appears under an item once its quantity is above 0, filled in with the item's price. It is your starting figure for the refund, and you can change it again when you resolve the claim.
- **Reason** - What went wrong, picked from your list of claim reasons, such as an item that arrived damaged or never arrived.
- **Note** - Anything your team should know, such as what the customer told you or what the photos show.

Click **New claim** at the bottom of the form. The claim appears on the order's **Claims** card with the status **Open**, its CLM number and the items you claimed for.

![A new claim showing as Open on the order's Claims card](/docs/images/user/orders/claims/2-new-claim.png)

A unit can be claimed again after a claim is denied or canceled. A second claim for a unit covered by an open, approved, or resolved claim is refused and nothing is created, so check the **Claims** card before you start.

The reasons on the form come from **Settings → Returns & reasons**, under **Claim reasons**. A reason you add there appears in the **Reason** list the next time you open a claim. See [<u>Returns & Reasons</u>](/user/settings/returns-and-reasons).

## How to Resolve a Claim

Every claim goes through a short set of steps, and the **Actions** menu (the three dots on the claim) offers only the steps that fit its current status.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Open** | The claim is recorded and waiting for your decision. No money or stock has moved. You can approve it or deny it. |
| **Approved** | You accept that something went wrong. Still no money or stock has moved, and the claim is waiting for you to resolve it. |
| **Resolved** | Settled with a refund or a replacement. The claim has no further actions. |
| **Denied** | Turned down. No refund and no replacement, and the claim has no further actions. |

1. On the claim, open **Actions** and choose **Approve**. The status changes to **Approved** straight away.
2. Open **Actions** again and choose **Resolve**. A box opens asking **How to resolve**.
3. Choose how to settle it and fill in the fields that appear, described below.
4. Click **Resolve**. The claim changes to **Resolved** and its **Actions** menu disappears.

![The Resolve claim box with Refund and replacement chosen, showing both the refund fields and the replacement switch](/docs/images/user/orders/claims/3-resolve-claim.png)

The **How to resolve** choice decides which fields you fill in:

- **Refund** - Asks for a **Refund amount**, filled in from the claim, and **Refund to**, which is where the money goes: **Store credit** or **Original payment**.
- **Replacement** - Asks which items to **Send a replacement for**, with a switch for each one.
- **Refund and replacement** - Shows the refund fields and the replacement switches together.

### Settling with a refund

Choose **Refund**, check the amount and pick where the money goes.

<Note>**Refund to** starts on **Store credit**. If the customer expects the money back on their card, change it to **Original payment** before you click **Resolve**.</Note>

With **Original payment**, the money goes back through the payment the customer used, and the order's payment total drops by the refund. The order's payment status then follows how much of it has been refunded: **Partially refunded** while some of the money is still with you, or **Refunded** once all of it has gone back. With **Store credit**, the amount is added to the customer's store credit balance, which you can see on their customer page, and their card is not touched.

<Warning>A refund on a claim is paid the moment you click **Resolve** in this box. Check the amount and the **Refund to** choice first.</Warning>

### Settling with a replacement

Choose **Replacement** and turn on the switch for each item you are sending again. The **Resolve** button stays greyed out until at least one switch is on.

When you click **Resolve**, no money moves. Instead a new fulfillment for the replacement item appears on the same order, with a delivery charge of $0.00 and a **Mark as fulfilled** button, and the order's fulfillment status changes to **Partial**. Sending out a fulfillment is covered in [<u>Processing Orders</u>](/user/orders/processing-orders).

## How to Deny a Claim

Deny a claim when you have looked into it and will not refund or replace anything. You can deny a claim while it is **Open**.

1. On the claim, open **Actions** and choose **Deny**.
2. A box asks you to confirm, warning that the customer will get neither a refund nor a replacement. Click **Deny**.

The claim changes to **Denied**, its **Actions** menu disappears and nothing on the order's payments changes.
