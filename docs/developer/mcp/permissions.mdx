An assistant acts for the person who connected it. There is no key to create or rotate: you sign in, tick the permissions you are willing to hand over, and those are its limits from then on.

## What you approve

Connecting takes you to your store, where a screen lists what the assistant has asked for. You tick what to allow. The names are the ones you already know from staff roles — products, orders, reports — so you are approving in the same terms you assign a colleague.

Three things are worth knowing:

**You cannot hand over more than you have.** If you cannot issue refunds, you cannot approve an assistant that can. Anything it asked for beyond your own permissions is simply dropped.

**It lasts until you take it back.** This is why your permissions are recorded as they are the day you approve. If you are promoted later, the assistant does not quietly gain what you gained.

**It is yours, not the store's.** Each person connects for themselves, and an assistant's actions are recorded against whoever approved it. Taking one person's access away leaves their colleagues working.

## What it can never reach

Some things are never offered, however much an assistant asks:

- **API keys, integrations and webhooks.** These create *other* ways into your store. An assistant that could make one could give itself permissions you never approved.
- **Roles and staff accounts.** For the same reason — an assistant cannot promote anyone, including itself.
- **Gift card and discount codes.** The code is the money. Anyone who reads one can spend it, so no assistant is shown them.
- **Erasing a customer.** It cannot be undone, and it should not sit behind a single confirmation. Do it in the dashboard.

## Two layers, not one

Approving a permission decides which jobs an assistant is **offered**. What each job then reaches is checked again, against the records themselves.

So an assistant approved for reports can run one — and will still be refused a report broken down by customer unless you also approved customers. And if your store limits a staff member to their own seller's products, an assistant connected by that person is limited the same way.

## Taking access away

**Settings → AI Agents** lists each assistant and the people connected through it, with what each person approved and when.

Revoking takes effect on the assistant's next request. The registration stays, so the same person can connect again later without an administrator setting it up afresh.

## Registering an assistant

Before anyone can connect one, the assistant has to be registered for the store — once, under **Settings → OAuth clients**. A registration grants nothing by itself; every permission is decided when a person connects.

## For things that cannot sign in

A nightly sync or a server-side integration has no person behind it and no browser to sign in with. Those use an [API key](/developer/cli/admin-api) instead, which is what keys are for. An assistant connection deliberately will not accept one.
