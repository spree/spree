Collections are published per channel with an optional schedule, mix rule-matched and hand-picked products, can exclude products, and take seven new rule types (category, product type, price, in stock, title, seller, custom field). The Admin SDK adds `collections.publications` and `collections.exclusions`; the Store SDK's `Collection` gains `image_alt` and `square_image_alt`.
