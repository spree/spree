List filters in the admin dashboard are quicker to narrow. Dropdown menus show checkboxes instead of trailing checkmarks, and every multi-select row offers "Only" on hover to keep just that value, or "Select all" to widen back. Quick filters show the colours of the statuses they let through and a count when narrowed. The Add filter panel lists values as checkboxes under an "Any …" row that clears the field, statuses can be picked several at once, and clicking a filter chip reopens the panel on that field. The Add filter button is now an icon with a tooltip.
